## Tips for Using These Templates

1. **Personalize** - Always replace [Client Name] and add specific observations about their business
2. **Choose the Right Template** - Use Template 1 for general outreach, Template 2 for busy clients, Template 3/4 for specific service needs
3. **Add Value** - Mention something specific about their business or industry
4. **Follow Up** - If no response in 3-5 days, send a brief follow-up
5. **Track Responses** - Keep notes on which templates work best for your audience
